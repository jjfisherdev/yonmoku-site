Yonmoku: 3D Four in a Row, by JJ Fisher LLC.

How to play. Two players share one phone or iPad. Hold a peg to aim, slide to choose, let go to drop. Four in a row wins: along a row, up a peg, or on any diagonal. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, double tap to return to the home view.

Resume. Leave a game at any time; the Home card shows Resume while a game is saved.

Settings. Sound, Ambience, Haptics and Drag the board are on the Settings card, from the Home card or during a match. Drag the board turns the board with your finger; off, a drag moves the camera the other way.

Accessibility. VoiceOver reads every control and the turn label; the text follows your Dynamic Type size; Reduce Motion shortens the camera moves.
