Privacy policyYonmoku: 3D Four in a Row

Yonmoku: 3D Four in a Row ("the app") is published by JJ Fisher LLC. Last updated 2026-10-08.

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software development kits. It makes no network connections of any kind.

The only data the app writes is one small save file on your device that holds the current game and series (the moves played, the score, and how long each move took) and your settings (sound, ambience, haptics, drag the board). This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

The app reads your device's accessibility settings (Reduce Motion, text size, VoiceOver) only to adapt its own display. It uses no sensor. Nothing is recorded or transmitted.
