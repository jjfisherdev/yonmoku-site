Privacy policyYonmoku: 3D Four in a Row

Yonmoku: 3D Four in a Row ("the app") is published by JJ Fisher LLC. Last updated 2026-11-09, for version 1.3.1.

The app has no account of its own, and JJ Fisher LLC does not receive any personal data from it.

Ads. From version 1.1 the app shows an occasional full-screen ad between games, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The ads are non-personalized and limited to content rated for all ages, and the app never asks for permission to track you. To serve the ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app sends Google device and usage information: an identifier for this app on your device, an approximate location derived from your network address, information about the ads you see and how they perform, and performance and crash diagnostics. Google's own policy describes what it does with this information: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads are personalized or your data is shared, and the Ad privacy choices entry in Settings lets you change it at any time.

Younger players. Where Apple reports that a parent must approve a significant change to an app, the app asks the parent through Apple's request and shows no ads until the parent approves; the game stays fully playable. For players Apple reports as under 13, Google's child-directed treatment applies to every ad request.

On your device. The app writes one small save file that holds the current game and series (the moves played, the score, and how long each move took), your settings (sound, ambience, haptics, drag the board, your choice of opponent), the names you give the two players, the record of series between players, the practice opponent's level, the days you solved the daily puzzle, the achievements you earned, and a small ad schedule (how many times and on how many days you have opened the app, how many games you have finished, and when the last ad appeared), so that ads appear only once you play often and stay occasional; it never leaves your device. They are deleted when you delete the app. A picture of the board you share is made on your device and goes only where you send it. The app reads your device's accessibility settings (Reduce Motion, text size, VoiceOver) only to adapt its own display, and uses no sensor.

Your iCloud. If iCloud is on, the record between players, the practice opponent's level, the solved puzzle days and the earned achievements are also kept in your own iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices show the same. JJ Fisher LLC cannot read them; Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Game Center. Only if you choose Achievements in Settings, the app asks Game Center to sign you in, and from then on reports the achievements you earn to your Game Center account. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; JJ Fisher LLC receives nothing from it.
